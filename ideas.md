# Selected Approach: Civic Ledger

## Design Movement

**Civic modernism meets editorial reporting.** The form behaves like a well-produced field report rather than a generic online questionnaire: composed information blocks, an asymmetric reading axis, grounded material colours, and a small sense of occasion.

## Core Principles

1. **Dignified clarity:** Every field is easy to locate, understand, and complete, with generous spacing and direct labels.
2. **Collective purpose:** Visual details refer to practical service, collaboration, and the many contributions that form a shared result.
3. **Editorial hierarchy:** Section numerals, short explanatory sentences, and varied typographic scale guide the student through the record.
4. **Responsive restraint:** Effects and motion improve orientation but never compete with a sensitive data-entry task.

## Color Philosophy

The palette begins with **parchment**—a warm neutral that makes a reflective, end-of-year document feel substantial rather than sterile. **SPPG green** is the ownable anchor: measured, authoritative, and optimistic. **Terracotta** indicates human contribution and feedback, while muted **marigold** signals progress or emphasis. Charcoal provides accessible readability; all inputs sit on an almost-white paper surface.

## Layout Paradigm

An **editorial field-sheet** layout: a persistent left-side project identity rail on desktop aligns with a wider, offset form column. Sections are presented as individual “entries” separated by fine rules and large sequence numbers, not as a centralised card stack. On small screens, the rail folds into the top header and the form becomes a single easy-to-scan column.

## Signature Elements

1. A **four-tile civic mark** with offset green blocks, suggesting connected individuals and projects.
2. A **vertical progress ledger** that marks completion status and provides an always-visible count of form sections.
3. Fine **contour-line textures** and small datum labels that make the page read like a designed field document.

## Interaction Philosophy

Controls should behave like precise tools: a visible active border, a light background shift, and immediate completion feedback. Multi-select answers use clear checked states and selected-item summaries. The user can save their work locally before submission, reinforcing trust for a longer reflective form.

## Animation

Use short 160–220ms `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` transitions only for focus, selection, button press, and section status. Form sections may softly fade upward on initial entry in staggered order; this is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No looping or decorative animation is permitted.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides thoughtful, authoritative headlines and section callouts. **Manrope** provides crisp, highly legible form labels, help text, and controls. Headlines use serif at 42–58px desktop / 34–40px mobile; labels use Manrope semi-bold at 13–14px with modest tracking; body text uses Manrope at 15–16px.

## Brand Essence

**A considered digital record for SPPG Nigeria fellows to document how their capstone service shaped a community.**  
Personality: **Purposeful, thoughtful, collective.**

## Brand Voice

Headlines are calm and affirmative. CTAs are practical and specific. Microcopy acknowledges the effort involved and uses simple, unambiguous language.

> “Record the work behind your community impact.”

> “Save your progress, then submit your year of service.”

Generic filler such as “Welcome to our website” and “Get started today” is not used.

## Wordmark & Logo

The logo is an **abstract civic tile**: four offset geometric blocks that meet around an open centre. It evokes many contributions joining to form a shared structure. It is paired with an intentionally spaced “SPPG / NIGERIA” wordmark in Manrope, never a default text treatment.

## Signature Brand Color

**Civic Green — #075F4E.**
